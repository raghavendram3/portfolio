Research areas

01

My work, in collaboration with experimental groups, uses computation to tackle challenges in sustainable catalysis.

Transition metal carbides

Design and characterization of Mo/W (oxy)carbides as efficient, non-noble metal catalysts for hydrodeoxygenation of biomass feedstocks. Identifying activity descriptors using ML for rational catalyst design.

DFTMKMSISSO

Mechanocatalysis

Surface-activated mechano-catalysis for ambient conversion of plastic waste into valuable hydrocarbons, exploiting mechanochemically generated radical intermediates at room temperature.

AIMDRadicals

Electrochemistry

Electrochemical bromination of bio-derived molecules such as methyl levulinate, understanding how solvent choice controls reaction selectivity.

SelectivityBio-derived

Machine learning for catalysis

ML potentials (MACE, fair-chem) and interpretable descriptors via SISSO for accelerating catalyst screening and understanding structure-activity relationships.
